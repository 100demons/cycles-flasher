3SAMPLE machine (Cycles One-Shot)

By durchgemacht · MIT license · this section is in English only for now

One extra machine, SAMPLE, plays a sample from the +Drive once per trig, the way the Model:Samples does: 6-tap interpolation at 96 kHz, then the Model:Cycles' own decimator and amp chain, and the Model:Samples' filter. The sample is chosen in the PRESET browser and saved with the sound, so projects, kits, presets and sound locks keep it. It is the Cycles One-Shot machine, built here from its own code at version 0.7.

  1. Put samples on the +Drive first: with Elektron Transfer, or with One-Shot's upload page (uploader/ in its repository), which works with the official OS too. Samples left there by the Model:Samples OS work as they are.
  2. Press MACHINES and pick SAMPLE, the 7th machine, after Chord. Its icon is Tone's.
  3. Open the PRESET browser (FUNC + MACHINE) on that track and open a sample file: the sound takes the sample's name and plays it. Hold a step while you open a sample and the step gets it as a sound lock.
  4. Shape the sound with the four knobs (table below). DECAY starts at 127 so a sample plays out; GATE is heard with a lower DECAY; PUNCH works as on Tone. PITCH, the keys and FINE set the pitch as on every machine: PITCH 0 at note 60 plays the sample as recorded, whatever its sample rate.
  5. The main screen shows the sample's name in a bar and its waveform across the screen; two lines mark where a trig starts and stops, and move as you turn COLOR and SHAPE.
KnobName on screenWhat it does
COLORStart (STRT)where the trig starts, 0 to 120 across the sample; moving it keeps the length
SHAPELength (LEN)how far it plays, 0 to 120: the trig ends at Start + Length, at most at the sample's end (120 plays to the end)
SWEEPCutoff (FREQ)the Model:Samples' filter: lowpass to the left (-64 closes it at 5 Hz), off at 0, highpass to the right (63 closes it at 19 kHz)
CONTOURReso (RESO)the filter's resonance, 0 to 127, up to a +27 dB peak at the cutoff
Sound
one voice per track, one shot per trig. No clicks at the edges: 4 ms fades at the end of what a trig plays, and at its start when Start lies inside the sample. The filter fades rather than switches across the centre, so a square LFO on Cutoff does not click; at Cutoff 0 the track is untouched, bit for bit. Stereo files play as mono; files at other rates keep their pitch.
Samples
the sample memory is 47 MB of RAM the Model:Cycles' OS leaves unused. Samples load in the background when a project, kit or sound names them, and unload when nothing does.
Limits
no looping and no reverse yet. The filter runs before Volume + Dist, where the Model:Samples has it after: with DIST above 0, the distortion acts on the filtered signal.
Other mods
goes with LiXR, Control All Config and the sequencer, screen, MIDI and USB mods. Not with MACRO, ACID, the Syntakt engines, Model-TG, the per-track filter or sample preview for now: those machines are not offered on this page yet, and Model-TG and the per-track filter change the same sound record functions.
Tested
One-Shot 0.7 on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-21): samples open and play, pitch, Start and Length, the filter, several SAMPLE tracks, saved projects, sound locks, the waveform view. This page builds One-Shot 0.8 composed from its compiled object by model27's registrar runtime, which differs from that firmware in how the machine is registered (a runtime reading a parameter block instead of detours assembled per build) and at three instructions (the machine's index read as a symbol). Flashed from this page, that build was tested in the digiemu emulator and on a Model:Cycles (2026-10-22); the same runtime also ran there with a second machine next to SAMPLE.

Before flashing another choice or the official firmware, set your SAMPLE tracks back to an original machine (Kick to Chord): the official OS doesn't have SAMPLE, and a project that still names it can freeze its MACHINES page.

13RecoverySecours

If the Model:Cycles no longer starts, its startup menu can always reinstall the OS: it lives in an area that updates never write.Si le Model:Cycles ne démarre plus, son menu de démarrage peut toujours réinstaller l'OS : il vit dans une zone que les mises à jour n'écrivent jamais.

  3. Send the official OS file through a USB-MIDI interface connected to the MIDI IN.Envoyez le fichier d'OS officiel par une interface USB-MIDI reliée au MIDI IN.

Nothing happens on the Model:Cycles screenRien ne se passe sur l'écran du Model:Cycles

Fast method: step 3 must say that the Model:Cycles was found. Classic method: check that CONFIG › UPGRADE is open and waiting. Either way, pick the port named “Model:Cycles” and close Elektron Transfer, Overbridge and your music software (on Windows they lock the port), then try again.Méthode rapide : l'étape 3 doit indiquer que le Model:Cycles a été trouvé. Méthode classique : vérifiez que CONFIG › UPGRADE est ouvert et en attente. Dans les deux cas, choisissez le port nommé « Model:Cycles » et fermez Elektron Transfer, Overbridge et votre logiciel de musique (sous Windows, ils bloquent le port), puis réessayez.

The fast method doesn't find the Model:Cycles, or stopsLa méthode rapide ne trouve pas le Model:Cycles, ou s'arrête

Turn the Model:Cycles on normally (not in CONFIG › UPGRADE), close Elektron Transfer and click Refresh. If it still fails, choose Classic in step 3: slower, but it needs no answer from the machine.Allumez le Model:Cycles normalement (pas dans CONFIG › UPGRADE), fermez Elektron Transfer et cliquez sur Rafraîchir. Si ça échoue encore, choisissez Classique à l'étape 3 : plus lent, mais sans besoin de réponse de la machine.

The transfer stops, or the Model:Cycles shows an errorLe transfert s'arrête, ou le Model:Cycles affiche une erreur

Turn it off and on and flash again. With the classic method, open CONFIG › UPGRADE again and first set Advanced › Send speed margin to 2.0 in the flasher.Éteignez-le puis rallumez-le et relancez. Avec la méthode classique, rouvrez CONFIG › UPGRADE et réglez d'abord Options avancées › Marge de vitesse sur 2.0 dans le flasher.

No MIDI port in the listAucun port MIDI dans la liste

Use a USB cable that carries data, turn the Model:Cycles on, then click Refresh. Some systems only show a new device after the browser is restarted.Utilisez un câble USB qui transporte les données, allumez le Model:Cycles, puis cliquez sur Rafraîchir. Certains systèmes n'affichent un nouvel appareil qu'après un redémarrage du navigateur.

MACHINES freezes on a trackMACHINES gèle sur une piste
